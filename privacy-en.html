Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

LED Display Solutions Hub ("the App") is a PWA-based engineering calculation tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All calculations and project data are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and never leave your device.

2. Permissions

The App does not request any device permissions beyond what is necessary for basic PWA functionality (offline caching via service worker).

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with any third party.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
